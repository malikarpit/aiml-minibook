AI/ML mein mathematics ko bina intuition ke ratna sabse badi galti hai. Har formula ke peeche ek simple physical ya geometric idea hota hai: Dot product ka matlab hai alignment, Matrix multiplication ka matlab hai space transformation, Bayes' Theorem ka matlab hai evidence aane par belief update, Chain Rule ka matlab hai gradient backpropagation, aur Gradient Vector ka matlab hai steepest ascent direction.
